# Canonical Example: The Dry Well

A complete 5-room adventure built using the Hollow Reach 2-Hour Adventure Build Guide.

---

## 1. Rumor & DM Notes

**Rumor:**
- **Location:** Stillwater Hollow (hamlet west of Wayfare)
- **Hook:** The well’s gone dry. Something below is groaning.
- **Reward:** Coin + civic favor from Mayor Andrel of Wayfare.

### What Happened
The well in Stillwater Hollow was originally dug decades ago and struck a natural aquifer at about 30 feet. For generations, it sustained the hamlet. Unbeknownst to anyone, the aquifer sat atop a sealed reliquary—a subterranean shrine of an ancient, now-forgotten deity.

Recently, a sinkhole opened beneath the well shaft, breaching the reliquary’s ceiling. The water now drains into this hidden chamber, slowly filling it. That’s why the well ran dry.

### Complication
The reliquary wasn’t just sealed for structural reasons. It held a bound spirit—a shade of avarice, the corrupted echo of the god’s final priest. Exposure to fresh water is awakening it.

### What the Mayor Did
Mayor Andrel sent a steward to investigate quietly before alerting Wayfare officials—afraid of losing prestige or support. The steward went down, found something, and died under suspicious circumstances (pushed or compelled to fall). The mayor is covering it up.

### Mystery Threads
1. The well is dry because the reliquary is draining the aquifer.
2. The reliquary is cursed—waking the shade.
3. The mayor is hiding what happened.
4. The players must decide: seal the reliquary? Cleanse it? Or expose the truth?

### Map Information
- **Procgen Generator:** Watabou One-Page Dungeon
- **Seed & Tags:** `seed=1738200248&tags=wet,small` (Hall of the Demon God)
- **Map Concept:** Subterranean reliquary situated at the center of a sunken lake at the bottom of the well. Overrun by aquatic decay. The entrance hall is the dry well breach.

## 3. Encounter Keys

### 🔽 Entry Shaft – “The Breach” (Bottom Corridor)

**DM Info:**
- **Dimensions & Space:** 5-ft-wide shaft dropping into a 15-ft by 30-ft chamber with a 7-ft ceiling.
- **Terrain & Lighting:** Unlit, slick masonry with pooled water. Archway west, wooden door east.
- **Acoustics:** Wind groans down the shaft; echoing water drips.

> **Sensory Read-Aloud:**
> - **Sight:** You descend a 5-foot-wide shaft ringed with smooth masonry that gives way to ancient carved stonework. Dust motes swirl in lantern light. A waterlogged boot is wedged in rubble.
> - **Sound:** The rope creaks ominously above. A low, rhythmic groan echoes from the darkness.
> - **Smell:** Stagnant water and faint scorched lantern oil.
> - **Touch:** Slick, cold stone and damp air.

**Gated Observations:**
- **Passive Perception 12:** You hear a low groan, but it is unclear if it is the wind or something deeper. Scrapes on the wall indicate a body was dragged or fell hard.
- **Investigation DC 12:** The boot bears a steward's crest (`H.A.` — Halric Aven). A shattered, rusted lantern lies in a puddle; the oil is burned away.

**Interactive Objects:**
1. **The Dangling Boot:** Wedged in rubble, carries the steward's crest.
2. **Crumbled Wall Stone:** Conceals an iron ring originally used for lowering buckets.
3. **Old Pulley System:** Rusted into place; can be unstuck with oil or Athletics DC 13 to create an escape winch.

---

### 🏛️ Room #1 – Hall of Thirst (Bottom-Left) — *Guardian / Threshold*

**DM Info:**
- **Dimensions & Space:** 25-ft square chamber, 15-ft vaulted stone ceiling aged with cracks.
- **Terrain & Lighting:** Cold, ankle-deep water covering the floor. Unlit.
- **Hazards & Exits:** Slippery floor (Acrobatics/Athletics checks at disadvantage). North iron-bound door is locked (Lock DC 20 / Break DC 20, or bypassed via basin ritual). Open archway east.

> **Sensory Read-Aloud:**
> - **Sight:** Seven veiled stone statues stand vigil around a dry basin carved into the floor. At the far end, a spectral figure in rusted plate rests its hands on a corroded greatsword, watching silently.
> - **Sound:** Subtle, dry whispers when the chamber falls quiet.
> - **Smell:** Mineral-heavy silt and old copper.
> - **Touch:** Ankle-deep, freezing water lapping against your boots.

**Gated Observations:**
- **Passive Perception 13:** Soft whispers murmur when the room is silent. One statue's watery reflection doesn't match its physical pose.
- **Investigation DC 13:** A hinged panel on the statue conceals a ruined scroll tube. The central dry basin is carved with the inscription: *"Only the unburdened may drink."*
- **Insight DC 14:** The wraith is bound to duty rather than raw malice—it awaits a confession.

**Tactics & Creature:**
- **Sword Wraith (CR 2):** Mobile / Sentry. Whispers: *"Speak a truth."* If lied to or attacked, it becomes immediately hostile. Can bonus-action teleport between the seven statues.
- **Morale Engine (50% HP):** If reduced to $\le$ half HP, demands a solemn oath or true regret to cease combat.

**Interactive Objects:**
1. **Iron-Bound Door:** Locked DC 20, Break DC 20, or unlocks automatically when the basin ritual is completed.
2. **Hinged Statue:** Conceals a scroll tube (contains a ruined rite prayer).
3. **Dry Basin:** Glows faintly if a character touches the water and speaks a genuine truth, regret, or confession; triggers the door mechanism.

---

### 🖼️ Room #2 – Chamber of Reflection (Middle-Left) — *Puzzle / Challenge*

**DM Info:**
- **Dimensions & Space:** 20-ft by 20-ft intimate shrine room, 10-ft ceiling.
- **Terrain & Lighting:** Water slowly pools across the cracked flagstones. Dim ambient glow.

> **Sensory Read-Aloud:**
> - **Sight:** A detailed mural spreads across one wall depicting pilgrims pouring water with bowed heads, while one figure drinks alone in defiance. A crude heart is freshly carved into the stone below.
> - **Sound:** Steady, rhythmic water dripping from ceiling cracks.
> - **Smell:** Chipped stone dust and fresh mineral chalk.
> - **Touch:** Damp walls with cool condensation.

**Gated Observations:**
- **Passive Perception 12:** Faint muddy bootprints lead directly to the heart carving. A rusted iron cup rests beneath the mural.
- **Investigation DC 12:** The heart was carved recently and clumsily (matching the steward's tools). Beneath a loose flagstone lies a dry holy symbol snapped cleanly in half.
- **Insight DC 13:** The mural depicts an ancient betrayal. The lone drinker matches the central statue in Room #1.

**Interactive Objects:**
1. **The Defiant Mural:** Can be consecrated, defaced, or cleansed with water to reveal hidden glyphs.
2. **Carved Heart:** Resonates faintly with necromantic energy; responds to Arcana or blood.
3. **Rusted Cup:** Mundane iron chalice placed as an offering; rings clear when tapped.

---

### 💀 Room #3 – Drowned Ledger (Top-Right Alcove) — *Setback / Complication*

**DM Info:**
- **Dimensions & Space:** 15-ft by 20-ft collapsed alcove.
- **Terrain & Lighting:** Knee-deep foul water. Partially buried beneath ceiling rubble.

> **Sensory Read-Aloud:**
> - **Sight:** Water deepens here and turns foul. Debris and fallen masonry bury the torso of a figure; one pale hand sticks out above the water, tightly clenched.
> - **Sound:** Gurgling drain noises from beneath the collapsed stones.
> - **Smell:** Rotting stagnant silt and decay.
> - **Touch:** Muck clinging to legs; water temperature drops noticeably.

**Gated Observations:**
- **Passive Perception 14:** A sealed cylindrical scrollcase glints beneath the murky water near the wall.
- **Investigation DC 14:** The corpse belongs to Halric Aven (the missing steward). He was stabbed in the back with a dagger before being dumped. His clenched hand holds dry ink, broken chalk, and a half-burned note: *"It wasn't supposed to go this deep."*
- **Insight DC 13:** Halric was eliminated suddenly by someone he trusted—Mayor Andrel's conspiracy.

**Interactive Objects:**
1. **Halric Aven's Corpse:** Can be examined, moved (requires DC 12 Athletics to clear rubble), or searched.
2. **Wayfare Scrollcase:** Sealed with Mayor Andrel's private wax crest; contains the ledger proving the mayor knew of the reliquary and had Halric killed to keep it quiet.
3. **Loose Floor Stone:** Hides Halric's family signet ring and a pouch with 25 gp.

---

### 🧿 Room #4 – Ritual Alcove (Bottom-Right) — *Secondary Discovery / Failsafe*

**DM Info:**
- **Dimensions & Space:** 15-ft by 15-ft hexagonal alcove.
- **Terrain & Lighting:** Dry elevated dais; floor runes pulse with faint violet light.

> **Sensory Read-Aloud:**
> - **Sight:** An altar of dark volcanic stone stands beneath a faded fresco of a chained, weeping god. A large cracked mirror leans against the corner, reflecting a distorted version of the chamber.
> - **Sound:** A slow, pulsing hum like a subterranean heartbeat.
> - **Smell:** Dried sandalwood incense and ozone.
> - **Touch:** The air here is noticeably warm compared to the flooded chambers.

**Gated Observations:**
- **Passive Perception 12:** The cracked mirror reflects the room without water, and shows the party members looking noticeably older and burdened.
- **Investigation DC 12:** A hidden drawer in the altar holds consecrated incense, powdered bone, and a broken brass sealing rod. The floor runes form a binding circle that can be activated to weaken the shade in the vault.
- **Insight DC 15:** This chamber was built as a failsafe—a sanctuary to choose between redemption, banishment, or sealing.

**Interactive Objects:**
1. **Volcanic Stone Altar:** Contains ritual implements; usable for consecration checks (Religion/Arcana DC 12).
2. **Cracked Mirror:** Shows alternate truths; gazing into it with a DC 13 Wisdom check reveals the Allip's true vulnerability.
3. **Pulsing Floor Runes:** Can be completed with chalk/blood to grant the party advantage on saving throws in the final battle.

### ⚰️ Room #5 – The Flooded Vault (Top-Center) — *Climax / Conflict & Reward*

**DM Info:**
- **Dimensions & Space:** 35-ft diameter grand circular vault, 25-ft domed ceiling.
- **Terrain & Lighting:** Knee- to waist-deep water. Flickering, unnatural dim light reflecting off ripples.
- **Acoustics:** Every water droplet reverberates like a sinister whisper.

> **Sensory Read-Aloud:**
> - **Sight:** The grand ceiling arches overhead. In the center of the flooded pool rests a massive stone sarcophagus, its lid cracked open. A golden chalice sits on a submerged dais corner.
> - **Sound:** Whispers ripple across the water's surface, echoing half-remembered names from your past.
> - **Smell:** Ozone, cold iron, and grave rot.
> - **Touch:** Numbing cold water pushing against your movement.

**Gated Observations:**
- **Passive Perception 14:** The whispering voices are calling out the characters' personal regrets. Something pale stirs just beneath the water's surface behind the sarcophagus.
- **Investigation DC 15:** The sarcophagus bears the same ancient seal found on Halric's ledger. Spiral runes were clawed into the interior stone from the inside out.
- **Insight DC 14:** The guilt lingering in the room is not ambient—it is being projected by an intelligent, starving entity feeding on remorse.

**Adversary Roster & Boss:**
- **Shade of Avarice (Corrupted Allip / Specter Variant, CR 3):**
  - **Tactics:** Stays submerged or phased through the central sarcophagus. Uses *Howl of Babble* / *Psychic Whispers* targeting anyone carrying the steward's ledger.
  - **Morale Engine (50% HP):** If reduced to half HP or when confronted with genuine redemption / truth spoken aloud from Room #1 or #4, screeches in agony and attempts to bargain: offers the secrets of other reliquaries in exchange for freedom.

**Layered Trapped / Locked Reward:**
- **The Golden Chalice of the Chained Deity:**
  - **Secret:** Submerged beneath the dais. Discovered via passive perception or sweeping the floor.
  - **Trap:** A warding siphon drains warmth from whoever touches it without speaking the ritual phrase (*"I drink no more than my share"*), dealing 2d8 cold damage (DC 13 Con half).
  - **Reward:** Chalice worth 150 gp, 80 sp, and a *Potion of Water Breathing*.

## 4. Post-Adventure Complications & Ripple Effects

Use these to fuel the Wayfare Discord rumor board and player After-Action Reports:

1. **💧 The Water Is "Off":** The well water returns to Stillwater Hollow, but villagers report guilt-ridden nightmares after drinking it. A water diviner in Wayfare declares: *"The flow is restored, but the water remembers."*
2. **🧾 The Mayor's Favor Comes With Strings:** If the party covers for Mayor Andrel, he pays handsomely (bonus 50 gp each) but now considers the party complicit. A messenger delivers an anonymous note in Wayfare: *"We know what you found. Name your price to stay quiet."*
3. **🐍 Another Well Runs Dry:** A week later, another hamlet east of Wayfare reports their well went dry overnight—forming a geographic ring of ancient reliquaries.
4. **🧠 The Allip's Echo Lingers:** The PC who struck the final blow hears whispering echoes when near bodies of water (minor roleplay flaw or 1/day advantage on Insight vs lies).

## 5. Buffer Ambush: Ambush at Dowsing Bridge

*Trigger:* Used if the session completes in under 2 hours.

- **Location:** Dowsing Bridge, narrow stone bridge crossing a stream east of Stillwater Hollow.
- **Enemies:** Silencers of the Hollow (1 Cult Fanatic + 2 Cultists or Shadowhounds sent by the Mayor's conspirator).
- **Tactical Terrain:**
  - *Felled Tree across road:* Half cover, or Athletics DC 12 to roll it into enemies.
  - *Bridge Railings:* Three-quarters cover from ranged attacks.
  - *High Ground:* Low hill with brush granting advantage on ranged attacks and perception.
  - *Overhead Rocks:* Athletics DC 14 to dislodge boulders (2d6 bludgeoning, restrained 1 round).
- **Encounter Intent:** Chokepoint skirmish. Enemies target the PC carrying the ledger. Players can win through combat, intimidation, or using the terrain to flee to Wayfare.

# Hollow Reach Dressing, Tactile Mechanics & Adversary Tactics

A comprehensive toolkit for generating interactive, tactically dynamic 5-room West Marches adventures.

---

## 1. Environmental Dressing & Improvised Stunts

Every keyed room MUST contain at least **three tactile, interactive props** from these tables that players can exploit, weaponize, or manipulate.

### 🪑 Common Furniture & Market Goods
- **Barrels & Crates:** Half cover; can be rolled down ramps (Athletics DC 12, 1d8 bludgeoning + knocked prone); pushed to create impromptu barricades.
- **Heavy Tables & Benches:** Flipped for three-quarters cover (requires Action or bonus action with Athletics DC 10); laid across chasms or water to bridge gaps.
- **Rugs & Tapestries:** Torn down to smother flames or blind enemies (Dexterity contest); conceals pit traps or hidden wall seams.
- **Curtains & Room Dividers:** Concealment; can be ignited with a torch to create a 10-ft smoke obscurement screen for 2 rounds.
- **Mirrors:** Distraction; redirect lantern light/lasers; peek around corners without exposing sight lines.
- **Carts & Wheelbarrows:** Mobile half cover; filled with debris and rolled to make noisy diversions or breach locked doors.

### 🌾 Storage & Trade Goods
- **Sacks of Grain / Flour / Rice:** Slashed open to create a 10-ft radius cloud of heavy obscurement (disadvantage on attack rolls; extinguishes open flames or creates explosion hazard if exposed to fire).
- **Lantern Oil Jugs:** Shattered on the floor (10-ft square); ignited with fire for 1d4 rounds (1d6 fire damage per turn) or left oily (DC 12 Acrobatics or fall prone).
- **Coils of Rope & Netting:** Dropped from above (DC 12 Dex or restrained); strung across hallways as tripwires (DC 13 Perception to spot, DC 12 Dex or fall prone).
- **Blankets & Burlap Bolts:** Used to smother fire, muffle alarm bells, or mask metallic armor clatter.

### 🔧 Workshop, Forge & Mine
- **Anvils & Counterweights:** Gravity drop traps (DC 13 Dex save, 3d6 bludgeoning, pins target until DC 15 Athletics check).
- **Smith's Tongs & Churns:** Improvised bludgeoning weapons; used to handle cursed or boiling artifacts safely.
- **Hot Coals & Forges:** Shove/bull-rush target into coals (DC 12 Athletics, 2d6 fire damage + catches fire).
- **Buckets of Water / Brine:** Splashed on hot metal to generate blinding steam clouds (10-ft radius obscurement).

### 🍗 Tavern, Kitchen & Larder
- **Iron Skewers & Meat Hooks:** Thrown as improvised javelins (1d6 piercing) or set into wooden doorframes to bar doors.
- **Boiling Cauldrons / Stewpots:** Tipped onto charging foes (10-ft cone, DC 13 Dex save or 2d6 fire damage).
- **Beer Kegs & Wine Casks:** Rolled into chokepoints; smashed open to create slick puddles.
- **Spices & Chili Powder Pouches:** Flung into eyes (5-ft range, DC 12 Con save or blinded until the end of next turn).

### 📚 Library, Reliquary & Study
- **Tall Bookshelves:** Tipped over onto enemies (Athletics DC 13, 10-ft line, DC 12 Dex save or 2d6 bludgeoning and restrained under books).
- **Ink Pots:** Hurled at eyes or splashed across smooth flagstones to make slippery oil-like zones.
- **Scroll Racks & Map Tables:** Ignited for quick fire hazards; smashed for improvised shields (+1 AC for 1 round).
- **Candles & Brazier Stands:** Flung as ranged fire attacks (1d4 fire) or used to detonate flour/gas clouds.

### 🏰 Religious, Ritual & Occult Spaces
- **Censers on Chains:** Swung like reach flails (1d6 bludgeoning) or dropped to emit heavy consecrated smoke.
- **Holy Water Basins:** Splashed onto undead/fiends (2d6 radiant) or used in purification/truth rituals.
- **Stone Statues & Effigies:** Pushed over onto enemies or mechanisms (Athletics DC 14); eyes contain sockets for gems or lever triggers.
- **Prayer Mats & Kneelers:** Conceal pressure plates, trapdoors, or hidden inscriptions underneath.

### ❄️ Cold Climate, Swamps & Outdoors
- **Hanging Icicles & Stalactites:** Dropped by striking ceiling (DC 12 ranged attack, 2d6 piercing damage).
- **Snowbanks & Silt Mounds:** Conceals footprints or hides traps; difficult terrain for medium creatures.
- **Felled Logs & Brush:** Half cover; can be set ablaze or shoved into water to create floating bridges.
- **Frozen Pools:** Slippery ice (DC 10 Acrobatics when dashing); breaking ice plunges target into freezing water (DC 12 Con save against exhaustion).

## 2. Tactile Challenge Design: Secrets, Locks & Traps

**Core Principle:** *Rolls are lifelines for when player problem-solving needs a boost, not barriers to play. Clues are tangible, interactions are tactile, and solutions require player agency or teamwork.*

### 🪨 The Layered Secret
- **Clue (Sensory lead-in / Passive Perception):** Give the sensory detail openly (e.g., *"You notice faint, parallel scratch marks in the stone behind the torn tapestry"*).
- **Interaction (Tactile Experimentation):** The secret requires physical manipulation (e.g., *"There is a recessed stone stud in the mortar, but pressing it alone produces only a dull clunk"*).
- **Solution (Multi-step / Teamwork):** The physical logic makes sense (e.g., *"The secret door pops open only when one player holds the stud depressed while another lifts the loose baseboard"*).
- *When to roll:* If players are stuck or ask to search blindly, a DC 12–14 Investigation check provides a direct hint to the mechanism.

### 🔐 The Layered Lock
- **Clue:** The lock is not an abstract DC number; it has physical telltales (e.g., *"The keyhole is rusted shut, but the brass corner rivets have clean finger grease on them"*).
- **Interaction:** Players test the object (e.g., *"Prying the lid fails, but twisting the top-right rivet causes a faint internal click"*).
- **Solution:** A tactile sequence (e.g., *"It requires two hands: depress both opposite rivets simultaneously while easing the latch forward. If released unevenly, the spring resets"*).
- *When to roll:* If a character uses Thieves' Tools without deciphering the trick, a DC 15 check picks it, but failure jams the pins.

### 🪤 The Layered Trap
- **Clue (The Warning):** Describe the telltale sign immediately (e.g., *"As your foot lands, the center paving stone sinks an inch with a soft hiss, and you spot narrow horizontal slits along the opposite wall"*).
- **Trigger Condition:** Clear mechanical trigger (e.g., *"Lifting weight off the stone releases spring-loaded spears"*).
- **Player Agency & Choices (No blind save sentences):**
  - *Freeze in place:* Maintains pressure; buys time for party to inspect.
  - *Dodge dive:* Roll Dexterity save with advantage if forewarned.
  - *Counterweight:* Slide a 50 lb backpack or iron shield onto the stone before stepping off.
  - *Jam mechanism:* Jam iron spikes or daggers into the wall slits (DC 12 Sleight of Hand / Thieves' Tools).

### 🎁 The Trapped, Locked Secret (The Capstone Treasure Box)
Ideal for the **Reward Room (Room 5)**:
1. **Layer 1: The Secret:** Concealed under a loose dais stone discovered by following scuff marks (Passive Perception 13+ or careful search).
2. **Layer 2: The Trap:** Lifting the coffer directly pulls a hair-thin wire attached to a poison needle or gas vial. Spotting it requires an Investigation DC 12; bypassing it requires sliding a flat blade under the base to sever the counter-spring.
3. **Layer 3: The Lock:** No keyhole—the lid is a puzzle knotwork ring requiring two runes to be pressed in harmonious sequence while turning the outer bronze rim.

## 3. Dynamic Adversary Roster & Combat Rules

### The 30/20 Combat Formula
To calibrate combat encounters for a 2–4 hour session:
$$\text{Max Boss CR} = \frac{3}{2} \times \text{APL} \quad (\text{Average Party Level})$$
$$\text{Guardian CR} = 1.0 \times \text{APL} \quad \text{to} \quad 1.25 \times \text{APL}$$

**Quick Level Bands for Hollow Reach:**
- **Greenhorns (Levels 1–2):** Guardian CR 1–2; Boss CR 2–3 (with 2–3 minor minions CR 1/8).
- **Adventurers (Levels 3–4):** Guardian CR 2–3; Boss CR 4–5 (with environmental terrain advantage).
- **Veterans (Levels 5–7):** Guardian CR 5–6; Boss CR 7–9 (with dynamic legendary actions or terrain hazards).

### Adversary Action Groups (Dynamic Dungeon)
Monsters never sit waiting passively in static rooms. Assign each creature/group to one of three categories:
- **Patrol:** Sentries following a specified circuit between 2–3 rooms. Roll 1d6 each exploration turn; on a 1–2, they advance to the next node.
- **Mobile (Default):** Stationed at their lair/room, but will investigate loud noises, thunder spells, or battered doors from adjacent rooms after 1–2 rounds. (Base 1-in-6 chance per 10-minute exploration turn to wander into adjacent hallway).
- **Stationary:** Physically bound to a room (chained beasts, automatons, sealed undead, trapped spirits). They do not pursue beyond their chamber threshold.

### Intelligent Morale Engine (Bloodied $\le$ 50% HP)
*No intelligent enemy fights to the death without overwhelming ideological compulsion.*
When an intelligent adversary drops below 50% HP:
1. **Pause Attack / Demand Parley:** Offers coin, hidden reliquary locations, or reveals Mayor/rival secrets in exchange for mercy.
2. **Tactical Retreat:** Uses Disengage, smoke, or environmental traps (dropping portcullises, kicking over oil) to fall back toward allies.
3. **Minion Defection:** Mercenaries or bullied underlings drop weapons or flee if their leader falls or flees.

### Environmental Combat Synergy
Enemies ALWAYS exploit their home turf:
- Goblins / Skulkers: Hide behind half cover, pop out to shoot, bonus action hide.
- Aquatic / Amphibious: Drag armored PCs into deep water (Athletics grapple contest).
- Undead / Spirits: Phase through stone walls or statues; immune to cold/exhaustion hazards present in the room.
- Cultists: Threaten to destroy evidence, ignite oil slicks, or trigger self-destruct collapses.
